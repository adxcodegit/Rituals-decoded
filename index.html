How we judge

Every claim is graded on its own, never the ritual as a whole. A ritual can have no physical effect and still carry real meaning; the grades keep those two questions apart.

Does it still matter?

Source policy

  1. Primary texts first, in reliable translation, with chapter and verse where we could check it.
  2. Standard scholarship such as Kane's History of Dharmasastra, then peer-reviewed science and systematic reviews.
  3. Regulators and courts (CPCB, NGT, High Courts, Supreme Court) for harms and legal status, checked live on the date shown.
  4. Never as evidence: forwarded messages, astrology sites or uncited "Vedic science" blogs. They appear only as examples of the myth.
  5. If we cannot verify it, we say so. You will see "No reliable source found" rather than a guess, and a C grade.
  6. Encyclopedias and news reports are labelled as such, so you can weigh them below peer-reviewed work.

About this project

What this is

A reference that separates what is historically documented, what science supports, what is plausible but unproven, and what is later rationalisation. It is meant for anyone: a curious student, a sceptical engineer, a devout grandparent, a visitor. It is not a pro-Hindu or anti-Hindu site. The same standard applies to claims that flatter the tradition and to claims that criticise it.

Who made it

Project by Aditya Nair, researched and built with Claude. Every source listed was opened and checked on the "last verified" date shown in each entry.

Current limits

  • 104 entries are researched. About a third rest on thin literature: we found no reliable study or text either way, and those entries say so.
  • Primary-text dates and verses are verified only where an entry says so; many origin fields read "not verified for this entry" until a text is checked.
  • Practice varies by region, sect, caste and family. Entries describe common forms and note where meaning changes.
  • Several primary texts are cited through public translations; some dates for texts are contested and are marked as such.
  • Several fast-moving legal questions (Sabarimala, PoP idol immersion) were checked on 29 October 2026 and may have changed since.
  • Nothing here is medical advice. Entries touching health include a note to consult a doctor.

Found an error?

Every entry has a "Report an error" button that prepares a short report with the entry, the claim and your source, ready to copy and send to the editor.
